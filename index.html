About the data in this tool

The compatibility guidance here is general industry knowledge (platform/immobiliser generation, standard blade & Lishi references) plus publicly available notes on Autel/Xhorse coverage. Autel and Xhorse both add, change and occasionally remove vehicle functions with firmware/software updates and paid licenses — for any bench or all-keys-lost job, especially on newer or premium vehicles (VAG MQB49+, BMW F/G-series, Mercedes FBS3/4, JLR, PSA/Renault), confirm the live function list in your tool's app or the vendor's current coverage list before you commit to a customer. Treat every verdict here as a strong first read, not a final guarantee.
